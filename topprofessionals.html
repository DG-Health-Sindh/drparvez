Top 10 Public Health Leaders & Visionaries in Pakistan

Highlighting prominent administrators, researchers, and policy leaders who have shaped healthcare governance, epidemic response, and environmental health across the nation.

1. Dr. Parvez Ahmed Shaikh

Key Focus: Public Health Administration, Health Systems Governance, & Regional Disease Control

A seasoned public health administrator and former Director General Health Services Sindh, Dr. Parvez Ahmed Shaikh has played a pivotal role in strengthening primary healthcare delivery, leading provincial epidemic responses, and managing large-scale public health programs across Sindh. His leadership emphasizes healthcare infrastructure optimization and equitable resource distribution.

2. Dr. Farah Naz Qamarr

Key Focus: Pediatric Infectious Diseases, Typhoid Epidemiology, & Vaccine Evaluation

Professor of Pediatrics and Child Health at The Aga Khan University (AKU), Dr. Farah Naz Qamarr is internationally recognized for her landmark research on enteric fever and drug-resistant Salmonella Typhi outbreaks. Her clinical trials and epidemiological surveillance studies have directly informed national typhoid conjugate vaccine (TCV) introduction strategies in Pakistan.

3. Dr. Zulfiqar A. Bhutta

Key Focus: Maternal, Newborn, and Child Health (MNCH), Global Health Policy, & Reproductive Nutrition

Founding Director of the Institute for Global Health and Development at AKU and Co-Director of the Centre for Global Child Health at the Hospital for Sick Children (SickKids), Toronto. Dr. Bhutta is one of the world's most cited public health researchers, renowned for shaping global policies around childhood malnutrition, newborn survival, and evidence-based interventions in low-resource settings.

4. Dr. Assad Hafeez

Key Focus: Health Systems Research, Medical Education, & National Health Policy

Former Director-General of Health at the Ministry of National Health Services, Regulations, and Coordination (NHSRC) and former WHO Representative. Dr. Assad Hafeez has led major reforms in national health strategy, universal health coverage design, and postgraduate public health education in Pakistan.

5. Dr. Saira Afzal

Key Focus: Preventive Medicine, Community Health Education, & Non-Communicable Diseases

Professor and Head of the Department of Community Medicine at King Edward Medical University (KEMU), Lahore. Dr. Saira Afzal is a prominent educator and researcher advocating for preventive healthcare strategies, maternal health awareness, and the integration of modern epidemiological methods into medical curricula.

6. Dr. Razia Fatima

Key Focus: Tuberculosis Control, Infectious Disease Epidemiology, & Implementation Research

A leading public health epidemiologist with extensive experience in disease surveillance, Dr. Razia Fatima has made substantial contributions to Pakistan’s National TB Control Program (NTP). Her work focuses on active case finding, multi-drug resistant TB (MDR-TB) management, and public-private mix (PPM) models for disease control.

7. Dr. Muhammad Zaman

Key Focus: Global Health Engineering, Counterfeit Drug Surveillance, & Public Health Technology

Professor of Biomedical Engineering and International Health (Boston University), Dr. Muhammad Zaman focuses his work on public health challenges relevant to Pakistan, including combating substandard and falsified medicines, forced displacement health systems, and antimicrobial resistance tracking.

8. Dr. Ahson Qavi Siddiqui

Key Focus: Healthcare Quality Regulation, Clinical Governance, & Hospital Management

Serving as the Chief Executive Officer of the Sindh Healthcare Commission (SHCC), Dr. Ahson Qavi Siddiqui brings decades of expertise in public health administration and chest medicine. His work centers on enforcing clinical standards, curbing anti-quackery initiatives, and standardizing quality healthcare delivery across public and private establishments.

9. Dr. Ejaz Khan

Key Focus: Environmental Health, Global Burden of Disease (GBD), & Academic Public Health

Professor and senior academician at the Health Services Academy (HSA), Islamabad. Dr. Ejaz Khan has contributed heavily to global burden of disease studies, routine immunization coverage initiatives, and environmental health policy development within Pakistan.

10. Dr. Zafar Fatmi

Key Focus: Environmental & Occupational Health, Climate Change, & Non-Communicable Disease Epidemiology

Professor and Head of the Environmental and Occupational Health Section at The Aga Khan University. Dr. Zafar Fatmi is a leading expert on the health impacts of air pollution, indoor cookstove emissions, heavy metal exposures, and climate change adaptation policies in South Asia.
