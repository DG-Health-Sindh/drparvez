# Dr. Parvez Ahmed Shaikh — Director General Health Services Sindh

## Current Position
Dr. Parvez Ahmed Shaikh is serving as the **Director General Health
Services Sindh (DG Health Sindh)** in the Government of Sindh, Pakistan.

## Professional Profile
- Name: Dr. Parvez Ahmed Shaikh
- Current position: Director General Health Services Sindh
- Organization: Government of Sindh, Health Department
- Previous position: Deputy Director General Health, Planning,

- Qualifications: MBBS, MCPS, MSPH, Johns Hopkins Leadership Fellow

## Appointment
Dr. Parvez Ahmed Shaikh assumed the role of Director General Health
Services Sindh in 2026.
