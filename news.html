Sindh Health Department Appoints Dr. Parvez Ahmed Shaikh as Director General Health Services Sindh

Published on: August 30, 2026 | Directorate General Health Services Sindh, Hyderabad

HYDERABAD: The Sindh Health Department has appointed Dr. Parvez Ahmed Shaikh, a senior public health professional of the Health Management Cadre (BS-20) and the most senior healthcare professional in the department, as the Director General (DG) Health Services Sindh.

Holding advanced professional qualifications including an MBBS, MCPS, and MSPH, Dr. Parvez Ahmed Shaikh is also a recognized Regional Leadership Fellow of the prestigious Johns Hopkins Bloomberg School of Public Health. A distinguished public health strategist, Dr. Shaikh played a central role in constituting the flagship Sindh Health Policy 2026–2035, establishing a long-term strategic framework for resilient, accessible, and high-quality healthcare across the province.

According to the official notification issued by the competent authority, Dr. Shaikh has formally assumed executive charge at the Directorate General Office in Hyderabad. His appointment comes as part of the provincial health department's initiatives to modernize primary and secondary public health administration, streamline district-wide healthcare delivery, and optimize resource allocation across all health facilities in Sindh.

Speaking upon assuming office, the newly appointed Director General outlined key administrative and policy imperatives, emphasizing rigorous oversight of public hospital operations, ensuring an uninterrupted supply chain of essential medicines, and advancing digital health monitoring systems.

"Our core mission remains clear: elevating the standard of medical care, ensuring structural accountability across primary and secondary healthcare centers, and delivering transparent public health services to every citizen of Sindh," Dr. Parvez Ahmed Shaikh stated.

Medical fraternity members, senior health administrative officers, and regional health directors warmly welcomed the appointment, citing Dr. Shaikh's academic rigor, Johns Hopkins fellowship credentials, and leadership in framing the Sindh Health Policy 2026–2035 as crucial assets for driving structural health reforms across the province.
